# Changelog

Each section is a version of the mod, the `version` in `mod.json`. The release workflow copies the
section of a tagged version into the release notes.

## 1.0.0

First version, from a player's suggestion.
- Two Special items: the Randoseru makes its wearer Immature, the Adult Card takes Immature's
  penalty away. Each works once, when equipped, and vanishes when taken off; the effect stays.
- A refused equip says why: no free skill slot, already Immature, not Immature.
- Before the third world, a very rare find in Malgrid Dungeon chests from the 5th stratum on, a
  little less rare deeper; from the third world on, Frostbuy sells both for 50,000 Gold.
